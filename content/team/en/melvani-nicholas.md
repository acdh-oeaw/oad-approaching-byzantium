Nicholas Melvani studied Byzantine Archaeology and Art History at the University of Athens and is currently Research Associate at Johannes Gutenberg University in Mainz with the Project “Approaching Byzantium”, funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG). He has taught classes in Byzantine Art and the topography of Constantinople at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul and at Mainz University. His publications include the monographs *Late Byzantine Sculpture* *(*Turnhout: Brepols International Publishers, 2013*) and* *Politics, Conflict and the Monastic Topography of 15th-Century Constantinople* (London: Routledge, 2025), as well as numerous articles on Byzantine epigraphy and on the topography of Constantinople, especially during the late periods and the transition to the Ottoman period.[](https://www.osteuropa.geschichte.uni-mainz.de/nicholas-melvani/)
